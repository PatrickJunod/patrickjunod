## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Fullstack Developer, UX/UI Designer & Product Owner** based in the heart of Switzerland. I've been passionate about the web since I was 13, when I landed my first gig as a webmaster — and I haven't stopped since. I co-founded a design agency at 17, worked at Celgene and Swisscom, and currently lead web development & product ownership at **Seekers Sàrl**.

I believe great software is built at the intersection of **solid engineering** and **thoughtful design**. I'm equally comfortable writing clean PHP, architecting a Laravel backend, crafting a reactive Vue interface, or shaping a product roadmap.

- 🏢 **Product Owner & Lead Developer** @ [Seekers Sàrl](https://www.seekers.ch), Marin-Epagnier, Switzerland
- 🤝 **Open-Source Contributor** to the [Statamic CMS](https://statamic.com) ecosystem
- ✍️ Occasional blogger at [patrickjunod.dev/blog](https://patrickjunod.dev/blog) — Laravel, Statamic, JS tips & tricks
- 🌍 French speaker, Swiss precision, global mindset
- 🎖️ **Google Technology Trailblazer Award** (2010) · **Switch Junior Web Award** (2010)
- 🧾 **Magnolia Certified** Project Developer & Light Developer (2024)
